Position Definition (Key Purpose of the Position):
This role drives the next-generation analytics capability in APAC by leveraging AI and building analytics agents. The
position focuses on transforming traditional data analysis into scalable, AI-powered workflows, enabling faster insights
and better business decisions across e-commerce, retail, and distributor channels.
Key Result Areas:
1. Data Analysis & Business Insight
o Drive data-driven analysis across APAC (E-commerce, Retail, WS)
o Translate business questions into scalable analytical frameworks
o Proactively identify trends, risks, and growth opportunities through KPI monitoring
o Deliver actionable insights to support decision-making
2. AI-Driven Analytics & Agent Development
o Utilize AI/LLM tools to redesign end-to-end analytics workflows (data prep, modeling, insight
generation)
o Independently build and deploy AI-powered analytics agents (e.g., using Copilot Studio)
o Develop reusable AI workflows (e.g., automated reporting, anomaly detection, insight generation)
o Continuously explore and apply new AI capabilities to solve business problems at scale
3. Business & Data Ecosystem Understanding
o Build deep understanding of retail, e-commerce, and distributor KPIs
o Work with SAP and multiple data sources to structure and integrate datasets
o Collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders to align data logic and usage
4. Communication & Influence
o Deliver clear and structured insights to regional and global stakeholders
o Translate complex analysis into business-friendly narratives
o Drive adoption of AI-based analytics within business teams
